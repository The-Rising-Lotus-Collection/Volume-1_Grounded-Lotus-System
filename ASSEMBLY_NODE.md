## 🔬 SECTION 2: NODE ASSEMBLY (THE UPGRADED UNIVERSAL HANDHELD MAGIC EYE)

### 2.1 Overview & Scope
The Node Assembly details the exact material layers and precision manufacturing steps required to fabricate the standalone, universal Handheld Magic Eye Tomography Node utilizing a 6-inch silicone funnel mold.

### 2.2 Component Specifications
*   **Core Element:** 6-Sided Vertical Tapered Vogel Quartz Crystal (1.0-inch protruding tip).
*   **Focus Matrix:** Three Pre-Cured 3-6-9 Volumetric Scaling Quartz-Epoxy Rings.
*   **Shielding Horizon:** Flat, Non-Gradient Round Copper Honeycomb Mesh Plate and a Small Toroidal Base Discharge Coil.
*   **Interface:** Six Vertical Female Connection Pins spaced exactly 60° apart inside a 6-magnet permanent static flux loop handle dock.

### 2.3 Pre-Cured Sub-Assembly Protocols
1.  **3-6-9 Ring Flash-Sealing:** Pre-cure the three tiered quartz-epoxy structural rings under compression to lock in their 15 MPa baselines. Apply a micro-thin **Vitrimer Pre-Flash Seal Coating** to block capillary slurry migration from entering their pores, keeping their internal density gradients untainted.
2.  **hBN Ceramic Pin Isolation:** Encase the six vertical female connection pins inside thin, non-conductive **Hexagonal Boron Nitride (hBN) Ceramic Sleeves**. 
3.  **Internal Path Routing:** Route the hBN-shielded pins vertically past the internal copper honeycomb mesh plate and the small toroidal base discharge coil. Ensure zero physical contact to completely eliminate capacitive crowding and high-voltage arcing paths.

### 2.4 Handheld Funnel Core Casting & Resonant Ticking
1.  Mount the 6-sided tapered Vogel crystal along the center axis of the 6-inch silicone funnel mold. Allow the narrow transmitter apex to protrude exactly **1.0 inch** out the bottom of the funnel throat to serve as the scanning face.
2.  Suspend the flash-sealed scaling rings around the crystal's natural facets (large ring at the wide base, medium ring at the mid-shaft, small ring near the tip profile) to focus acoustic fields along the longitudinal axis.
3.  Pour the active thixotropic quartz-epoxy slurry over the internal framework to fill the 1.0-inch cross-sectional hull depth.
4.  **The 10-Second Resonant Pulse:** Connect acoustic transducers to the node mold frames. Throughout the chemical curing cycle, fire a **70.47 Hz pulse precisely every 10 seconds**.
5.  **The Physics Goal:** This continuous acoustic wave front thwarts direct polymer cross-linking against the flat, polished facets of the Vogel crystal, forming a controlled micro-aperture air gap that isolates the crystal from curing contraction forces.

### 2.5 Rear Gland Assembly & Quality Control
1.  Set the rear handle dock with the 6-magnet permanent static flux loop array.
2.  Verify the hBN-shielded female pins align perfectly with the Class-1 Universal Puck and Info Puck standard male connectors for wireless data distribution.
